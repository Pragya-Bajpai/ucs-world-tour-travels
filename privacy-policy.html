Privacy Policy

At UCS World Tour & Travels, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information when you use our website and travel services.

1

Introduction

Welcome to UCS World Tour & Travels. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information while providing travel-related services.

2

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide while using our website, making enquiries or booking travel services, including:

  • Name and contact details.
  • Email address and mobile number.
  • Travel preferences and booking information.
  • Payment and transaction details.
  • Other information voluntarily provided by you.
3

How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to process travel bookings, provide customer support, send booking confirmations and updates, improve our website and communicate important service-related details.

4

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

5

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience, understand website usage and enhance website functionality. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

6

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party providers for payment processing, booking services and website functionality. These providers may handle information according to their own privacy policies.

7

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with relevant service providers when necessary to complete your bookings or comply with applicable legal requirements.

8

Your Privacy Rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or assistance regarding your personal information, subject to applicable laws.

9

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy whenever necessary. Any changes will be published on this page with the revised information.

10

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact our team.

Need Help?

If you have any questions about our Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us.
